# Rencore Governance: selection and scope

Checked 24 October 2026. Family: microsoft-365-workspace-governance, absent from the rebuild registry before this run. It is distinct from access recertification, software subscription cost tracking and generic privacy request handling: this build centres on workspace ownership, stale activity, sharing, retention and dated evidence.

## Bill and six scores

GBP 14,382.10 a year is the arithmetic annualisation of a historical GBP 43,146.31 three-year contract for 500 Rencore Governance Enterprise licences, published 13 February 2024. [Procurement record](https://www.stotles.com/tenders/2024/W7/rencore-governance-enterprise-licences). This is a secondary record of a public-sector award, not a current vendor tariff, current NZ/AU invoice or 10-50 person benchmark. No conversion to dollars is invented. The public page therefore uses no numeric price.

| Criterion | Score / 5 | Evidence or limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Annual bill | 2 | Historical five-figure annualised GBP contract. Current small-business invoice unverified. |
| Data-shaped | 5 | Workspace snapshots and review decisions support the bounded export-based review process. |

Total: 20/30. AG5 (1/5/2/2/5/4, 19/30) and Qualtrics Strategic Research (1/4/2/3/4/4, 18/30) were considered but the current public entry pricing found did not establish the required annual bill. AG5's pricing page now quotes privately. Qualtrics publishes a smaller entry research subscription. Neither was registered.

- [Reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/190608/Rencore-Platform/): older reviewers mention setup time, filter speed and desired automation improvements. These are dated individual experiences and are not asserted as current defects.
- [AG5 pricing](https://www.ag5.com/pricing/) and [Qualtrics trial/pricing](https://www.qualtrics.com/free-trial/) informed the rejected alternatives.

The live platform offers broad scanning, policy automation and Microsoft management that this bounded free base does not reproduce. The page offers a custom version scoped to the owner's needs. It does not imply full feature parity, guaranteed savings or that the vendor cannot answer the ten demonstrated questions. No public market-demand number, customer bill or rival comparison is invented.
